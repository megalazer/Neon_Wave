# SIX VOICES, ONE CITY

**Classification: OPERATIVE BRIEFING // Clearance: INDIGO**
**Distribution: NEED-TO-KNOW // Version: 2.1.7**

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

The city has no name. Not one that anyone agrees on, anyway. On a Lexicon census
form it is one string of characters; on a Grammaton patrol manifest, another;
on the tongue of a Static jammer running a dead relay in the Undercity, it is
just "here."

Geography is simple: coastal megapolis, sprawling inland from a rusting port,
layered forty floors up and an uncertain number down. The Undercity is not on
any map the surface powers will admit to keeping. The towers are older than the
corporations that own them. The rain is acidic three nights out of five.

Six factions control every layer. Language, law, signal, money, depth, and
noise. Every contract you take serves one of them, offends one or two more, and
pays in a currency that is never just credits. The factions do not govern the
city so much as they occupy adjacent realities on the same grid. When their
realities collide, people like you get paid.

Your fixer knows which voice is singing today. You should learn to hear them
all.

## 2. THE SIX FACTIONS

### GRAMMATON — Order / Enforcement

**Tag: GRAMMATON**
**Domain: law, order, enforcement, military contracts**

Rules are the only real things. That is the Grammaton creed, and they enforce
it with a rigor that borders on theology. They write the legal grammar of the
city and they are the only people who can read it aloud. Their enforcement arm,
the **Grammaton Enforcement Division**, handles military contracts and private
security for corporate clients who can afford the premium. The **Onyx Bureau**
runs city patrol, checkpoint operations, and low-level order maintenance. Both
answer to the same grammar.

Grammaton operatives move like punctuation: precise, final, and never
accidental. Their patrol units carry Grammaton Gen-2 keypad auth, and their
internal audit teams are slower than anyone would like but never wrong. A
Grammaton-sanctioned runner invokes the grammar itself; the badge is real and
the weight behind it is heavier.

They are not the government. They are the reason the government stopped trying.

Cross them, and you learn what it means to be an ungrammatical sentence. They
do not kill you. They edit you out.

*Sample contracts: REDLINE_ENFORCEMENT (retrieve rogue contractor's gear),
DEEP_COVER (plant fabricated identity inside Grammaton management).*

---

### LEXICON — Knowledge / Language

**Tag: LEXICON**
**Domain: knowledge, language, archives, biotech, media**

The Lexicon believes meaning lives in the body. They are gene-scribes, wetware
linguists, archivists of every word ever spoken in the city and every sequence
that codes a living thing. **Helix**, their biotech and media arm, runs the
billboards that cycle propaganda across the district skyline every evening.
Helix also holds the patents on most of the cyberware that keeps runners alive.
The line between their pharmaceutical division and their propaganda division is
a filing convention.

The **Lexicon Archive** is the sum of recorded knowledge, sealed and curated.
Getting a file into the Archive means it happened. Getting it out means you
have friends Lexicon does not know about.

Lexicon acolytes guard broadcast nodes the way Grammaton guards checkpoints:
with conviction. Their gene-cant is a dialect of certainty. When the Lexicon
speaks, it is not a suggestion. It is a definition.

*Sample contracts: THE_WORD_MADE_FLESH (heist a rival gene-scribe's research
suite), ASSET_EXTRACTION (extract a defecting Grammaton analyst and her data).*

---

### SIGNAL — Communication / Networks

**Tag: SIGNAL**
**Domain: communication, networks, transmission, infrastructure**

If a packet moved, the Signal saw it. Infrastructure as theology: that is the
Signal's entire position, and they own the pipes to back it up. Relay towers,
fiber trunks, the backbone grid that makes the city's nervous system twitch
when someone sends a credit or a scream or a lie.

**Signal Relay Corp** is the public face. The telecom fronts are less public.
They all answer to the same axiom: transmission is truth. A dead relay is a
kind of blasphemy. You do not leave the Signal's pipes broken.

Signal is nominally neutral. They sell bandwidth to everyone. But neutrality is
a product, and products have prices. The Signal knows whose packets are heavy
and whose are light. They keep the data. They always keep the data.

*Sample contracts: SIGNAL_TAP (intercept a corporate data stream), DEAD_RELAY
(restore a dark relay in the Undercity, find out who spliced the line),
BLACKWALL_PROBE (extract data from beyond the Deadwall ICE).*

---

### REFERENT — Commerce / Resources

**Tag: REFERENT**
**Domain: commerce, resources, energy, finance**

Value is whatever Referent says points to value. They are the finance bloc that
trades in meaning itself. The **Referent Exchange** sets the price of
everything, from synth-coin futures to the kilowatt-hour that keeps your
subdermal alive. Their energy and commodity holdings stretch across the city's
industrial spine.

Referent does not fight. They do not need to. Every faction pays its bills
through Referent-cleared channels, and every channel carries a spread. When
Referent wants a competitor's valuation model poisoned twelve hours before a
hostile bid, they hire runners who speak fluent money. When they want a rumor
planted in three exchange backchannels that moves a thin synth-coin so they can
short it, they hire runners who can wear a lie like a hot tip.

They will never be your enemy because they have already priced the cost of your
friendship.

*Sample contracts: MARK_TO_MARKET (corrupt a competitor's valuation model),
PRICE_DISCOVERY (seed a rumor to move a coin), VAULT_BREACH (crack a Helix
distribution warehouse for its contents).*

---

### UNDERTOW — Shadow / Secrets

**Tag: UNDERTOW**
**Domain: black market, smuggling, secrets, the gray economy**

The meaning under the meaning. Undertow is the gray economy beneath the
Undercity: smugglers, fixers, couriers who run crates that do not exist on any
manifest. They own the canals, the forgotten drainage culverts, the routes that
Grammaton's maps do not show.

Undertow jobs come through Nyx's people more often than not. Zero-trace
specialist. The work is always the same shape: keep it dry, keep it moving, do
not ask. A courier who quits mid-run is a courier nobody hires twice. A runner
who delivers clean to Undertow is owed in a currency deeper than credits.

When a ledger surfaces that names every fixer in the gray economy, Undertow
does not call just anyone. They call family. That door does not open twice.

*Sample contracts: LOW_TIDE_RUN (move a crate through three checkpoints
unlogged), PACKAGE_RUN (three-stop courier with no questions), THE_MEANING_UNDER
(steal the ledger of every fixer before Grammaton seizes it).*

---

### STATIC — Streets / Noise

**Tag: STATIC**
**Domain: streets, scavenging, anti-signal, jamming**

Noise as a creed. Static believes the only honest message is no message. Their
street crews are the city's gangs: territorial, loosely organized, and
dangerous in the way that people with nothing to protect are always dangerous.

Scrap-runners scavenge the underlayers. Signal-jammers spike transmitters until
they shriek feedback across every band. When a Lexicon broadcast node goes dark
mid-gospel, turning a million screens honest, Static calls it prayer.

They control passages nobody else wants. A Static cell holding a canal crossing
does not ask for credits. They ask for noise. Let them scramble your comms for
ten minutes. Blind and deaf but alive. Their crews fight with whatever is at
hand: scrap metal, stolen cyberware, the conviction that silence is the only
truth.

Static is not unified. It is not supposed to be. Organization is a kind of
signal.

*Sample contracts: KILL_THE_WORD (silence a Lexicon broadcast node mid-gospel
transmission), BURN_IT_DOWN (eliminate a rival gang supply depot in Rustline).*

## 3. THE RIVALRIES

**Lexicon versus Static.** Knowledge against noise. The Lexicon archives
meaning; Static jams it. Every Lexicon broadcast is an act of definition, and
every second of Static silence is a refutation. When Static overrides a
gene-gospel transmission with screaming feedback, Lexicon hears heresy. When
Lexicon billboards cycle their evening propaganda loop, Static sees an
invitation.

**Grammaton versus Undertow.** Order against shadow. Grammaton writes the
grammar of the city; Undertow lives in the clauses Grammaton cannot parse.
Every checkpoint is a sentence Undertow must slip between. When Grammaton
moves on a safehouse, Undertow moves faster. When Undertow loses a crate to a
patrol, Grammaton logs it as a correction.

**Signal versus Static.** Signal against jamming. The Signal's theology is
transmission; Static's is interruption. A dead relay in the Undercity might be
a fault, or it might be a Static splice bleeding bandwidth into the gray. The
Signal always wants its pipe back, and it always wants the answer to which.

**Referent stays neutral.** Everyone needs money. Referent sets the price of
bandwidth for the Signal, the price of silence for Static, the price of
enforcement contracts for Grammaton, the price of extraction for Undertow, and
the price of a Helix biotech derivative for the Lexicon. They do not take
sides. Sides are a market inefficiency.

## 4. IN-WORLD TERMINOLOGY

A runner's vocabulary maps the city more precisely than any street grid.

| When you mean... | In this city, you say... |
|---|---|
| Military contractor / private army | Grammaton Enforcement Division |
| City police / patrol | Onyx Bureau |
| Biotech conglomerate | Helix (a Lexicon holding) |
| Media / propaganda network | Lexicon Archive broadcast nodes |
| Telecom monopoly | Signal Relay Corp |
| Energy conglomerate | Referent energy holdings |
| Street gang | Static street crew |
| Black-market fixer network | Undertow's gray economy |
| Stock exchange | Referent Exchange |
| Cyberware manufacturer | Helix wetware division |

The fixers you work through are not faction operatives. They are brokers who
speak to every voice: **REMI_VANCE** (freelance, reliable, neutral), **PYRE_9**
(weapons-hot, likes collateral), **NYX_NULL** (ghost ops, zero trace),
**KADE_SYNC** (corporate extraction, pays well), **DUSK_7** (high-risk,
high-reward, fewer questions). Each has a channel to every faction. Each knows
whose voice is singing today.

The currency is credits. The work is multi-stage. You will plant taps in Signal
relays, crack Helix vaults for Referent buyers, smuggle crates through
Grammaton checkpoints for Undertow, and silence Lexicon broadcasts for Static.
Every job writes your name deeper into one faction's ledger and further from
another's.

Choose carefully. The city is listening.
